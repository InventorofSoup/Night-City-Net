Ed's Bravery Stew

Combine two Sunspike tomatoes, Moonstring beans, one cave onion, and any common river fish at an Elfhold cooking fire. Stir clockwise. The clockwise part may not matter, but mine has never wiped a party.

  • Feeds four characters
  • Minor fire resistance
  • Recipe persists through logout
